📋 Ingredients

Chicken Breast (boneless, skinless) Turkey Breast
200g
Brown Rice (cooked)
150g
Broccoli (steamed) Green Beans
100g
Olive Oil
1 tbsp
Garlic (minced)
2 cloves
Lemon Juice
1 tbsp

Salmon Fillet
180g
Sweet Potato (baked)
150g

100g

👨‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Season the chicken breast with salt, pepper, and your preferred spices. Let it marinate for 15 minutes at room temperature.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken breast to the pan and cook for 6-7 minutes per side, or until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. While the chicken is cooking, steam the broccoli for 5-6 minutes until tender but still crisp.
  5. Plate the cooked brown rice, add the chicken breast, and arrange the steamed broccoli on the side. Drizzle with lemon juice before serving.
